## WinDivert (`vendor/zapret/bin/WinDivert.dll`, `WinDivert64.sys`)

Packet capture library and driver by basil00, licensed LGPLv3 / GPLv2. <https://reqrypt.org/windivert.html>
Antivirus products sometimes flag it because it intercepts network packets; this is expected for zapret.
